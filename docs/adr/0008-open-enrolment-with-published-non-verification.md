# Anyone may publish a CapabilityProfile, and the platform says so

There is no eligibility check. A person claims to have lost their income in the 10 August 2026
earthquake and publishes a CapabilityProfile; nothing verifies the claim. **The decision is not
merely to skip verification — it is to state the absence prominently**, on the Wall and on every
CapabilityProfile, rather than letting a clean and sympathetic site imply a vetting that does not
happen.

## Considered options

**Requiring an RUD number** was rejected on a factual mismatch, not on friction. The Registro
Único de Damnificados records _housing damage_, assessed by Bomberos or Cruz Roja, and its
registration window closed on 26 August 2026. A receptionist whose building survived but whose
employer closed — the exact person this platform exists for — was never eligible for it.

**Partner vouching** (parroquias, juntas de acción comunal, fundaciones) remains the intended
path to stronger trust and is being pursued for other reasons. It was not made a precondition
because waiting on institutional relationships would put launch weeks past the window in which
attention, and therefore Hirers, exist at all.

**Manual review of every profile** was rejected because the reviewer is one person who is already
the queue for Offers, photos, and Reports.

## Consequences

**The platform's only trust signal is the absence of one.** Everything else that protects a person
is downstream of accepting that: every Offer is read by a human before delivery, standing safety
guidance sits on every profile and every Offer, contact details are released only at Contact
Exchange, and free-text fields reject phone numbers and email addresses so the consent step
cannot be routed around.

**Verification cannot be added quietly later.** Introducing it retroactively casts doubt on every
profile that predates it, so a future verified tier has to be additive and visibly dated rather
than a silent upgrade.
