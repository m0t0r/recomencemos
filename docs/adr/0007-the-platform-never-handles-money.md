# The platform never handles money

Recomencemos connects a Worker with a Hirer and stops. No escrow, no payment rail, no fee, no
held balance — the terms of an Offer are recorded as text, and every peso moves between two
people through channels this platform never sees.

## Why

Taking money would mean cross-border transfer into Colombia, and with it money transmission
registration, KYC/AML obligations, chargebacks, and a disputes desk. The operating team is one
unpaid person with no legal entity. A payments feature would not be a feature; it would be the
whole company, and it would arrive months after the earthquake it exists to answer.

## What we gave up, and are not pretending otherwise

**A Worker has no recourse through us if she is not paid.** We hold nothing, so we can refund
nothing and withhold nothing. The site says so plainly rather than implying a protection that
does not exist — an implied guarantee is worse than none, because it is relied upon.

**We cannot prove our own impact.** With no money flow there is no ground truth about whether a
Contact Exchange became income. The substitute is a check-in email to both sides seven days
later, which is self-reported and weak, and is the only evidence available. Any figure we publish
carries that qualification.

## Consequences

The check-in doubles as the safety signal that reporting misses: a Worker who answers "no me
pagaron" has told us about a bad Hirer without having to accuse anyone.

Adding payments later is not an increment. It changes the legal entity, the regulatory surface,
and the trust model in one move, and it would need its own decision rather than an extension of
this one.
